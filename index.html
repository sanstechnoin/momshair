Wisdom in Every Drop: Our Ingredients

We use only pure, cold-pressed oils and hand-picked herbs, just like our grandmother did. No chemicals, no mineral oil, no artificial fragrance. Ever.

Nourish & Shine Oil

  • 🥥 Coconut Oil: Deeply conditions and adds a natural, healthy shine.
  • 🌺 Hibiscus Flower: Rich in amino acids, it helps strengthen roots and reduce frizz.
  • 🌿 Amla (Indian Gooseberry): A powerhouse of Vitamin C, it promotes hair growth and scalp health.
  • 🌰 Almond Oil: Seals in moisture and adds softness, making hair manageable.

Anti-Grey Hair Oil

  • ⚫ Bhringraj (King of Herbs): Traditionally used to help slow premature greying and reduce hair fall.
  • 🌱 Curry Leaves: Packed with antioxidants that help maintain natural pigment and strengthen hair follicles.
  • ⚫ Black Sesame Oil: A warming oil used in Ayurveda to nourish the scalp and support natural hair color.
  • 🍃 Neem: Known for its clarifying properties, it helps keep the scalp healthy and free from dandruff.

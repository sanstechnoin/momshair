Daily Habit. Sunday Ritual.

Light enough for daily grooming in the Indian way, yet rich enough for a deep weekend massage.
Here is our recommended routine:

Daily: Light Touch

Apply a few drops daily to damp hair for shine and protection. Non-sticky and perfect for work or school.

Sunday: The Scalp Reset

Generously massage warm or cool oil into the scalp for 10-15 minutes. Boosts circulation and relaxation.

Let It Soak

For deep repair, leave it on for at least 1 hour (or overnight) before washing off with a mild cleanser.

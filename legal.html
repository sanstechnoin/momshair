Transparency & Trust

Privacy Policy

At Mom's Hair Oils, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This policy explains how we handle your data.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the essential information needed to process your order:

  • Name, Shipping Address, and Phone Number (for delivery).
  • Payment Confirmation Details (Screenshots/UTR) for verification.

2. How We Use Your Data

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal identification information to others. Your data is used strictly for:

  • Processing and shipping your orders.
  • Sending order updates via WhatsApp or Email.
  • Legal compliance and fraud prevention.

3. Data Security

Your data is securely stored on Google Firebase servers, which utilize industry-standard encryption and security protocols. Payment screenshots are used solely for verification purposes and are handled with strict confidentiality.

Terms of Service

1. Nature of Product (Handcrafted Variance)

Our products are 100% natural, sun-cooked, and handcrafted in small batches. Unlike factory-made products, you may notice slight variations in color, texture, or scent between different batches. This is a sign of purity and natural ingredients, not a defect.

2. Cosmetic Use Only (Medical Disclaimer)

This product is a herbal cosmetic preparation intended for grooming and hair care. It is not a medicine and is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease (such as Alopecia or other medical hair loss conditions). Results may vary from person to person.

3. Allergy Warning & Patch Test

Our oils contain natural ingredients (Coconut, Hibiscus, Fenugreek, etc.) which are generally safe. However, natural ingredients can cause allergic reactions in some individuals. We strongly recommend performing a patch test on your forearm before full use. We are not responsible for individual allergic reactions.

Shipping & Returns

1. Shipping Policy

We ship fresh batches weekly. Delivery typically takes 5-7 business days across India depending on your location. You will receive a tracking number via WhatsApp once your order is dispatched.

2. Cancellation Policy

Orders can be cancelled for a full refund if the request is made within 4 hours of placing the order (or before the shipping label is generated). Once the tracking number is generated/shipped, cancellations are not possible.

3. Return & Refund Policy

Due to the hygiene nature of personal care products, we do not accept returns or exchanges on opened or used bottles.

However, we offer a replacement if:

  • The bottle arrives damaged or broken.
  • You received the wrong item.

To claim a replacement, please send an Unboxing Video or clear photos to our WhatsApp support within 24 hours of delivery.
